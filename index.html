How the forecast works

Your day score (0–100) comes from each check-in: energy counts most, then mood, motivation, stress, and sleep quality (each 1–10). Any you skip are left out rather than guessed.

"Your usual" is your average over the last 28 logged days. Day types are relative to that, so a "gentle" day means lower than your normal, not a grade.

The forecast starts from your usual and adjusts for: recent sleep against your target (fading over a few days), recent momentum, training fatigue from the last four days (including sessions you plan ahead), weekday patterns in your own history, your cycle phase (projected from the period start dates you mark), and days you flag as big or stressful. As you log more, it learns how strongly sleep and training affect you specifically.

Accuracy is checked by replaying your history: each logged day is forecast using only the days before it, then compared to what you actually logged.

Data & settings

CSV columns (any order): date (YYYY-MM-DD), sleep_hours, sleep_quality, energy, mood, motivation, stress (1–10 each), training (rest / cardio / lifting), weight, tags (e.g. "Bloated; Cramps"), period (yes on the day a period started), note. Everything stays in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
