# Daily Forecast

A seven-day outlook for how you're likely to feel, built from your own daily check-ins — with a plan for each kind of day and encouragement when you need it.

- **Strong / Steady / Gentle** days, relative to *your* normal, not a grade
- Forecast adjusts for sleep, training fatigue (including planned sessions), weekday patterns, cycle phase, and flagged big days
- Learns how strongly each factor affects you as you log more
- Accuracy is measured honestly by replaying your history
- Log several entries a day (e.g. a morning weigh-in and an evening mood check); each is kept separately
- Five sections under the menu: Daily Forecast, Daily Check-in, Trends (scores, cycle history and forecast, weight), How the Forecast Works, Data & Settings

All data stays in the browser on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. Use **Export** to back it up. Data saved by the earlier one-entry-per-day version is migrated automatically on first load, including converting 1–10 ratings to 1–5 (1–2→1, 3–4→2, 5–6→3, 7–8→4, 9–10→5). The old copy is left in place as a backup.

**Install on your phone:** open the site, then Share → *Add to Home Screen* (iPhone, Safari) or ⋮ → *Install app* (Android, Chrome).

Not medical advice.
