Your day score (0–100) comes from your ratings: energy counts most, then mood, motivation, stress, and sleep quality. If you skip a rating, the score is built from the ones you gave. Appetite is tracked for your history but doesn't change the score, since more or less appetite isn't a better or worse day.

"Your usual" is your average over the last 28 rated days. Day types are relative to that, so a "gentle" day means lower than your normal, not a grade.

The forecast starts from your usual and adjusts for: recent sleep against your target (fading over a few days), recent momentum, training fatigue from the last four days you logged, weekday patterns in your own history, and your cycle phase (projected from the period start dates you mark). It's built only from what you've already logged. As you log more, it learns how strongly sleep and training affect you specifically.

Accuracy is checked by replaying your history: each logged day is forecast using only the days before it, then compared to what you actually logged.

Several entries in one day

Every save is its own entry, so a morning weigh-in and an evening mood check both stay on the record. For the day as a whole: ratings you give more than once are averaged; weight uses your first weigh-in of the day; hours slept uses the first value you log; training uses the hardest session you logged; the tags you pick under "Need a word?" and your notes are all kept.

A day only gets a score once you've rated energy or mood. Until then (say, after a morning weigh-in and sleep rating) the forecast keeps showing for that day.

Your cycle

Cycle length is the average gap between your last six period starts (gaps between 20 and 45 days; anything outside that is treated as a missed log). With fewer than two starts it assumes 28 days. Phases are estimated from that length: period (days 1–5), follicular, around ovulation (about 14 days before the next period), luteal, and premenstrual (the last five days). Estimated days and future cycles are shown lighter on the Trends chart.

Settings

Your data

Importing (JSON or CSV) adds entries to what's here and skips any that exactly match an entry you already have. Nothing you've logged is overwritten. Backups from the older 1–10 version are converted to 1–5 automatically.

CSV columns (any order): date (YYYY-MM-DD), time (HH:MM, optional), sleep_hours, sleep_quality, energy, mood, motivation, stress, appetite (1–5 each), training (rest / cardio / lifting), weight, tags (any "Need a word?" tag, e.g. "Bloated; Fatigue"), period (yes on the day a period started), note. Blank cells are left blank. Everything stays in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
